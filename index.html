Our Ritual

Coffee is more
than a drink.
It's a ritual.

We founded Aro on a simple conviction: great coffee should never be rushed. We travel directly to origin farms in Yirgacheffe, Huila, and Antigua to partner with growers who prioritize regenerative agriculture and unparalleled flavor.

Every lot is roasted locally in micro-batches to highlight terroir, sweetness, and complexity — then brewed with mineral-balanced water calibrated specifically for each harvest.

The Process

From Soil to Cup.

Three deliberate stages that define the Aro standard.

01

High-Altitude Sourcing

We select strictly high-grown Arabica (1,700m–2,200m ASL) where slow bean maturation yields concentrated sugars, vibrant acidity, and distinctive floral notes.

Geisha • Bourbon • Typica
02

Precision Roasting

Using infrared drum roasters with real-time rate-of-rise profiling, each lot is roasted gently to preserve the bean's intrinsic terroir without smoky bitterness.

Clean Profile Zero Char
03

Calibrated Extraction

Reverse-osmosis water remineralized with magnesium and calcium, dialed in with 0.1g dose precision and variable pressure profiling on bespoke espresso gear.

19.5% Ideal Yield
